# Home cooking and family help

Agreed with the owner on 2026-10-26, after the second behaviour review found
the town starving while residents talked about pots on stoves that didn't
exist, and relatives in other houses never looking out for each other. The
owner wanted real mechanics, not notes from nowhere. None of this text goes
into a prompt: residents find it all in-world.

## Home cooking

- **Where:** in your own house. Every house has a small stove.
- **Who:** anyone. The plain dishes need only cooking level 1, which everyone
  has. The skilled dishes (sambal kangkong) stay at the kopitiam.
- **What:** a small pot, one batch at a time, **2 servings a batch** (3 for a
  Master cook). The kopitiam's bigger batches and its skilled dishes keep it
  the place for volume.
  - bubur: rice and water
  - fish porridge: fish, rice and water
  - vegetable rice: vegetables and rice
  - kopi: coffee powder and water
  - **grilled fish** (new): two fish. It fills more than raw fish.
  - **boiled kangkong** (new): two bunches of vegetables. It fills more than raw.
- **Ingredients** come from what the cook carries, and the servings go back
  into their hands, to eat, give or sell.
- **Sharing a meal:** a cook holding food can serve hungry family or household
  with them, one serving each (Kampung, 2026-10-29, owner told). Offered only
  with real food in hand and someone hungry of theirs there; each eats it just
  as they'd eat it themselves.
- **Seeing it:** anyone near someone holding food sees it beside them
  ("Fatimah (…, holding 2 plates of boiled kangkong)"), and a hungry resident
  with nothing to eat is told what they could cook at home from what they
  really have (held, their own shop's stock, their house's stove and fuel),
  with the one missing thing named (Kampung, 2026-10-29).
- **Fuel:** a stove needs fuel for each batch.
  - **Firewood:** free. Gathered past the farm plot (a 20-minute chore, one
    bundle). One bundle cooks one batch.
  - **Kerosene:** bought from Pak Osman. A tin poured into a house's stove
    lasts four batches, for anyone cooking in that house. A stove with
    kerosene in it uses that before any firewood.
- **Time:** the same stove time as the kopitiam's dishes, and the cook is busy
  at the stove meanwhile.
- **Practice:** a batch finished at home counts towards cooking skill, as at the
  kopitiam.

## Family help (instead of notes from nowhere)

Residents already know their family (their own card says who). Two things now
follow from that knowledge in the game's own rules:

1. **The hungry think of their kin.** A hungry resident with nothing to eat,
   when no shop with food is being minded, thinks of a relative in another
   house before the jetty: "You're hungry. You could ask your uncle Rahim for
   something to eat."
2. **Kin remember a faint relative.** Seeing a relative nearby looking faint
   with hunger becomes a memory (once a day per relative), so it can shape
   what they do later, not only while they stand there.

Word passing from person to person (option 3) was not chosen.
