# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism
about the work, not the person. Harassment, slurs, doxxing and sexualised
content are not tolerated — and that includes sharing other people's personal
details.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer through GitHub: use the
**Report content** option on the comment or issue, or open a private security
advisory under the repo's **Security** tab and mark it as a conduct report.
Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove content, lock threads, or block participants who break
these rules.
